Our Story

Founded Where the Savannas Meet the Rainforest

We built Kewicare Safaris to give travelers direct, honest access to Uganda’s wildest places — without middlemen, inflated booking commissions, or generic corporate packages.

Born and headquartered in Kampala, Kewicare Safaris was founded by native Ugandans who spent their childhoods within reach of the Great Rift Valley, the foothills of the Rwenzori Mountains, and the verdant rainforests of western Uganda. For generations, foreign travelers paid steep markups to overseas brokers who had never walked a trail in Bwindi Impenetrable National Park or driven the tracks of Queen Elizabeth National Park. We started Kewicare to change that dynamic: to connect visitors directly with experienced local planners, vetted drivers, and certified rangers who know every seasonal animal corridor, every hidden river bend, and every rural community.

As a fully registered tour operator licensed by the Uganda Tourism Board (UTB) and recognized by the Uganda Wildlife Authority (UWA), we manage every facet of your itinerary from our central operations desk on Lugogo Bypass in Kampala. Our team works hand-in-hand with UWA headquarters to secure official, non-refundable gorilla trekking and chimpanzee habituation permits directly from the source. Whether securing permits for mountain gorilla expeditions in Bwindi — home to over 459 mountain gorillas, almost half the planet’s surviving population — or arranging private game drives across the 3,893-square-kilometer expanse of Murchison Falls National Park, we cut out third-party brokers so your investment goes directly into your travel experience.

Our overarching mission is simple yet uncompromising: to make Uganda’s untamed biodiversity and warm hospitality accessible to curious travelers worldwide, while ensuring tangible economic benefits reach the communities living alongside our national parks. Under Uganda’s national conservation model, 20% of all park entrance fees are channeled back to bordering communities to build health clinics, construct schools, and mitigate human-wildlife conflict. We deepen this impact by employing indigenous guides, hiring local porters on every trek, and partnering with eco-certified safari lodges that purchase their organic produce from neighboring villages. When you travel with us, your safari directly protects endangered species and empowers the people who safeguard them.

Discover how our team transforms your travel goals into seamless day-by-day itineraries by exploring our custom Uganda safari packages or reviewing our transparent, stress-free safari planning process.

Our Guiding Values

How We Operate Every Single Safari

Every decision we make — from the lodges we partner with to the routes we recommend — is driven by four core principles.

Radical Transparency

We believe hidden fees damage trust before a trip even begins. When you request an itinerary with Kewicare, you receive a transparent, line-item proposal showing exact park entry charges ($40/day standard UWA rate), gorilla trekking permits ($800 official foreign non-resident rate), accommodation meal plans, and private vehicle allocations. There are no surprise credit card processing fees, no currency conversion sleights of hand, and no mandatory undisclosed surcharges upon arrival.

Deep Local Expertise

Our safari guides and operational staff aren't temporary contractors — they are seasoned Ugandan professionals who grew up in the rural districts surrounding our national parks. They know the subtle signs of lion prides moving through the Ishasha sector of Queen Elizabeth National Park, understand the vocalizations of mountain gorilla families in Bwindi's four trekking sectors (Buhoma, Ruhija, Rushaga, and Nkuringo), and navigate regional roads with unshakeable confidence.

Responsible Tourism & Conservation

Tourism must protect fragile ecosystems and directly enrich local communities. We follow strict Leave-No-Trace principles, enforce safe distances during all primate encounters, and avoid single-use plastics across our vehicles. Beyond supporting UWA’s statutory 20% community revenue-sharing fund, we actively engage local porters on every gorilla and chimpanzee trek and patronize community-run cultural enterprises like the Batwa experience and Bigodi Wetland Sanctuary. Read traveler reflections on these encounters in our reviews section.

Mid-Trip Agility & Care

African travel is vibrant, dynamic, and occasionally unpredictable. Unseasonal rains can turn murram roads into slick mud, international flights can experience delays, or your group may choose to spend an extra hour photographing chimpanzees in Kibale Forest. Because our operations team is on the ground in Kampala, we pivot itineraries seamlessly in real time. We reschedule boat safaris, communicate with lodge management, and reorganize transport without leaving you stranded with automated support.

Common Questions

Everything You Need to Know About Us

Clear, direct answers about our licensing, permits, and how we operate in Uganda.

Is Kewicare Safaris an officially licensed tour operator in Uganda?
Yes. Kewicare Safaris is an officially registered and tax-compliant tour operator based in Kampala, Uganda, licensed by the Uganda Tourism Board (UTB). We maintain direct accreditation with the Uganda Wildlife Authority (UWA), granting us authorized access to purchase gorilla trekking, chimpanzee tracking, and national park entry permits on behalf of international travelers.
Where is your physical office and how do you support travelers during a trip?
Our headquarters are located in Kampala, Uganda. From Entebbe International Airport (EBB) to the remote borders of Kidepo Valley and Bwindi Impenetrable Forest, we manage all logistics locally. While traveling in Uganda, every guest is assigned a dedicated trip coordinator who is reachable 24/7 via phone and WhatsApp for immediate support, route modifications, or flight updates.
How do you guarantee a 100% gorilla permit success rate?
Mountain gorilla trekking permits are strictly capped by the Uganda Wildlife Authority at 8 visitors per habituated gorilla group per day (costing $800 per foreign non-resident). Because our operations team is physically based in Kampala minutes from UWA headquarters, we monitor real-time permit availability across all four Bwindi sectors (Buhoma, Ruhija, Rushaga, and Nkuringo) and Mgahinga Gorilla National Park, securing and locking in your permits immediately upon trip deposit.
What makes booking with Kewicare different from booking through an overseas agency?
Overseas booking agencies operate as middlemen — they mark up prices by 20% to 35% before outsourcing the actual ground logistics to a local Ugandan operator. When you book directly with Kewicare Safaris, you work straight with the local experts who drive the 4x4 Land Cruisers, inspect the safari lodges in person, and coordinate directly with park wardens. You pay local, transparent rates and receive immediate, firsthand knowledge.
