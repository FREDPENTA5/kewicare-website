Cancellation Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Home / Cancellation Policy

We understand that plans can change. This policy outlines the refund schedule and terms that apply when you need to cancel or modify a confirmed booking with Kewicare Safaris.

1. Cancellation Refund Schedule

Refunds are calculated based on how far in advance you cancel before your trip start date. All cancellations must be submitted in writing via email to info@kewicare.com.

Notice Period Refund Amount What You Forfeit
90+ days before trip 70% refund 30% deposit (non-refundable)
60–89 days before trip 50% refund 50% of total trip cost
30–59 days before trip 25% refund 75% of total trip cost
Less than 30 days No refund 100% of total trip cost
No-show No refund 100% of total trip cost

Refunds are processed within 14 business days of receiving your written cancellation request. Refunds are issued via the same payment method used for the original booking.

2. Gorilla & Chimpanzee Permits

Uganda Wildlife Authority (UWA) gorilla and chimpanzee permits are non-refundable and non-transferable once issued. This is a UWA policy that Kewicare Safaris cannot override.

  • If you cancel after permits have been purchased, the permit fee ($800 for gorilla trekking, $250 for chimp trekking) cannot be refunded.
  • In exceptional circumstances (medical emergency with documentation), Kewicare will request UWA to reschedule the permit to alternative dates, but this is not guaranteed.
  • If Kewicare has not yet purchased permits at the time of cancellation, the permit fee will be included in the refund calculation above.

3. Trip Modifications

If you want to change your trip rather than cancel it entirely:

  • Date changes (more than 60 days before trip) — no fee, subject to availability of lodges and permits.
  • Date changes (30–59 days before trip) — $50 administrative fee per change, subject to availability.
  • Date changes (less than 30 days) — treated as a cancellation and rebooking. The cancellation schedule above applies.
  • Itinerary changes (adding/removing parks, changing lodges) — accommodated where possible. Price differences apply. No fee for changes requested more than 60 days before departure.
  • Group size reduction — if one or more travelers drop out, the per-person cost may increase (especially for private vehicle safaris). Kewicare will provide a revised quote.

4. Force Majeure

If your trip cannot proceed due to events beyond anyone's control, including but not limited to:

  • Natural disasters, volcanic activity, or severe weather
  • Government travel bans or park closures
  • Pandemics or disease outbreaks
  • Political instability or civil unrest
  • Airline cancellations or airspace closures

Kewicare Safaris will work with you to reschedule your trip at no additional cost (subject to lodge and permit availability). If rescheduling is not possible within 18 months, we will issue a refund minus any non-recoverable costs already incurred (e.g., permits purchased, lodge deposits forfeited by the lodge).

5. Cancellation by Kewicare Safaris

If Kewicare Safaris must cancel your trip due to safety concerns, operational issues, or insufficient group numbers (for group departures only):

  • You will receive a full 100% refund of all payments made.
  • We will assist with rebooking at no extra charge if you prefer.
  • Kewicare Safaris is not liable for consequential costs such as international flights, visa fees, or travel insurance premiums.

6. Travel Insurance

We strongly recommend purchasing comprehensive travel insurance that includes trip cancellation coverage. Insurance can protect you from financial loss in situations where this cancellation policy does not provide a refund — including medical emergencies, flight disruptions, and unforeseen personal circumstances.

Kewicare Safaris can provide documentation to support insurance claims where applicable.

7. How to Cancel

To cancel or modify a booking, contact us in writing:

  • Email: info@kewicare.com
  • WhatsApp: +256 771 188630

Please include your booking reference, the name on the booking, and the reason for cancellation. We will confirm receipt and the applicable refund amount within 2 business days.

8. Questions?

If you have questions about this policy or need clarification before booking, don't hesitate to reach out. We're happy to walk you through the details.
