How We Build Your Uganda Safari

We take the friction out of East African travel logistics. Learn how our seamless 3-step turnaround delivers peace of mind from first contact to your final airport drop-off.

1. 24-Hour Consultation

Tell us your travel dates, budget preference (luxury, midrange, or budget), and must-see destinations. We verify current Uganda Wildlife Authority (UWA) permit availability for your target dates immediately.

2. 48-Hour Custom Itinerary

You receive an itemized, day-by-day itinerary proposal detailing private 4x4 transport, vetted safari lodges, English-speaking driver-guides, park entry fees, and all gorilla trekking permits. Discover our standard planning framework on our How It Works page.

3. Direct Permit Lock-In

Upon itinerary approval, a deposit secures your official UWA gorilla trekking cards and lodge reservations. We provide digital permit receipts and remain on standby 24/7 during your travels across Uganda.

Gorilla Permits & Trekking Sectors

Uganda is home to more than half of the world's remaining mountain gorillas in Bwindi Impenetrable National Park and Mgahinga Gorilla National Park. Official UWA permits are $800 per person for foreign non-residents ($700 for foreign residents). Permits sell out months in advance for peak dry seasons (June–September and December–February). For full sector details across Buhoma, Ruhija, Rushaga, and Nkuringo, visit our specialized Gorilla Trekking in Uganda page.
