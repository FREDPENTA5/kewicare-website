Frequently Asked Questions

Booking & planning queries, answered

Here are the common questions travelers ask about securing permits, booking deposits, itinerary adjustments, and arriving in Uganda.

How far in advance should I book my Uganda gorilla safari?

We strongly recommend booking 4 to 6 months in advance, especially if you intend to travel during the dry seasons from June to September and December to February. Mountain gorilla trekking permits in Bwindi Impenetrable National Park and Mgahinga Gorilla National Park are strictly capped by the Uganda Wildlife Authority (UWA) at 8 visitors per habituated gorilla family per day. During high season, permits sell out months ahead.

What deposit is required to confirm my booking?

To confirm your safari dates, we require a standard 30% deposit of the safari package plus 100% of the gorilla and chimpanzee permit fees. Permit fees must be collected in full upfront because the Uganda Wildlife Authority requires full payment to issue non-refundable permit certificates in your name. The remaining balance is payable 45 to 30 days prior to your arrival in Entebbe.

Can I make changes to my itinerary after paying the deposit?

Yes. We pride ourselves on custom flexibility. If you need to add an extra night at a safari lodge, swap a road transfer for a domestic flight, or adjust a game drive schedule, we will gladly adapt your itinerary subject to lodge availability. Official UWA gorilla permit dates can sometimes be rescheduled subject to UWA's official transfer policy and administrative fees.

What happens if my international flight into Entebbe is delayed?

Your assigned Kewicare safari concierge tracks your incoming international flight in real time. If your flight is delayed or rescheduled, your private driver-guide will be waiting at arrivals when you land, regardless of the hour, at no extra charge. If needed, we seamlessly adjust the first day of your safari schedule so your wildlife experience remains intact.

Are gorilla permits included in the quoted safari price?

Yes. Every Kewicare proposal that features gorilla trekking clearly itemizes and includes the official Uganda Wildlife Authority permit ($800 per person for foreign non-residents, $700 for foreign residents, and UGX 300,000 for East African citizens). We never conceal permit costs or add unexpected permit acquisition surcharges.

What payment methods does Kewicare accept?

We accept secure international bank wire transfers (SWIFT / SEPA) in USD, EUR, and GBP, as well as major international credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express) through our encrypted payment gateway. Full payment instructions and bank coordinates are supplied on your official invoice.

What is your cancellation and refund policy?

Cancellation policies depend on the timing of your notice and the independent terms of partner lodges and the Uganda Wildlife Authority. UWA gorilla and chimpanzee permits are generally non-refundable once issued, though they may be transferred to other eligible travelers. Full cancellation schedules and trip postponement options are detailed clearly in our booking terms.
