Questions, answered

The things you are probably wondering

Is it cheaper to book everything myself?

Sometimes the sticker price looks lower, but permits, park fees, and road transport in Uganda are easy to underestimate or double-book. Our pricing is upfront with no mark-ups after you book.

Do you handle visas and gorilla permits?

Yes. Visa guidance, gorilla and chimp permits, and park entry fees are arranged and confirmed before you travel.

What happens if my flight is delayed?

Your Kewicare contact is reachable 24/7 while you are in-country. Transfers and the first day of your itinerary are adjusted at no extra cost.

Can you build a custom trip for my group?

Yes. Tell us your dates, budget, and what you want to see and we will put together an itinerary built around your group pace.

Do I need to pay in full upfront?

No. A deposit secures your dates and permits, with the balance due closer to travel. Exact terms are shown at checkout.

What is your cancellation policy?

Cancellation terms depend on how close to departure you cancel, since permits and lodges have their own deadlines. Full terms are sent with your itinerary.
