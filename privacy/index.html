Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Home / Privacy Policy

Kewicare Safaris ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal data you share with us. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how we handle it when you use our website (kewicare.com) or engage with our services.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly

When you contact us, submit a trip request form, or book a safari, we may collect:

  • Name and email address
  • Phone number (including WhatsApp)
  • Travel preferences — dates, group size, trip type, budget range
  • Passport details — only when required for gorilla permit applications or visa processing, and only after you have confirmed a booking
  • Payment information — processed through secure third-party providers; we do not store card numbers

Information collected automatically

When you visit our website, we automatically collect:

  • Device and browser information — browser type, operating system, screen resolution
  • Usage data — pages visited, time on site, referral source
  • IP address — used for analytics and approximate geographic location

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal data to:

  • Plan your trip — build itineraries, secure gorilla permits through UWA, book lodges and transport
  • Communicate with you — respond to inquiries, send itinerary proposals, provide travel updates
  • Improve our website — understand how visitors use kewicare.com so we can improve the experience
  • Legal compliance — meet obligations under Ugandan law and UWA permit requirements

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services that may process your data:

  • Google Analytics (analytics) — collects anonymized website usage data. Google Privacy Policy
  • Formspree (contact form processing) — receives form submissions. Formspree Privacy Policy
  • Vercel (website hosting and analytics) — serves website content. Vercel Privacy Policy
  • Uganda Wildlife Authority (UWA) — receives passport details solely for gorilla and chimpanzee permit applications

4. Cookies

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies for:

  • Essential functionality — ensuring the website works correctly
  • Analytics — Google Analytics uses cookies to understand visitor behavior

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some website functionality.

5. Data Retention

  • Trip inquiry data — retained for up to 24 months after your last interaction, then deleted
  • Booking and travel records — retained for 7 years as required for tax and legal compliance in Uganda
  • Passport details — deleted within 30 days after your trip ends, unless required by law
  • Analytics data — retained by Google Analytics for 14 months

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal data, including:

  • Encrypted connections (HTTPS) on our website
  • Restricted access to personal data within our team
  • Secure handling of passport and payment information

No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction — ask us to update inaccurate information
  • Deletion — request that we delete your personal data (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Objection — opt out of marketing communications at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at info@kewicare.com.

8. Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal data, we will delete it promptly.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of our website after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
