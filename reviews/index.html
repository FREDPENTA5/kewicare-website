Why our safari reviews stay 5-star

Uganda is one of the most rewarding safari destinations on Earth, but complex logistics require true on-the-ground expertise. Here is how Kewicare Safaris ensures every journey runs smoothly.

UWA-Certified Local Guides

Our driver-guides are born and raised in Uganda, certified by the Uganda Wildlife Authority (UWA) and trained in defensive driving, wildlife ecology, and multi-language guest care.

Guaranteed Gorilla Permits

We source gorilla permits direct from the UWA desk in Kampala months ahead ($800 official foreign non-resident fee), securing your preferred trekking sector before dates sell out.

24/7 In-Country Support

Our Kampala dispatch office is on call 24 hours a day. Flight delays, weather rerouting, or spontaneous itinerary tweaks are handled immediately by phone and WhatsApp.

Custom 4x4 Safari Vehicles

Every private safari travels in high-clearance Toyota Land Cruisers fitted with wide pop-up observation roofs, individual charging outlets, onboard mini-fridges, and VHF emergency radios.

Personally Inspected Lodges

We don't rely on stock photos. Our team inspects every lodge and tented camp in person to guarantee cleanliness, exceptional food, hot water pressure, and breathtaking views.

Transparent All-Inclusive Quotes

Your quote includes all park entry fees, ferry crossings, game drives, ranger guides, taxes, and specified accommodations. Never any surprise add-ons or hidden markups.

Reviews & Booking FAQ

Frequently asked questions about our safaris

Everything you need to know about our customer reviews, booking guarantees, and how we handle Uganda travel logistics.

How does Kewicare verify reviews and traveler testimonials?

Every review featured on our site comes from verified travelers who completed a private or small-group safari with Kewicare Safaris. Following every trip, our team conducts a formal post-safari review and checks guest ratings across Google, TripAdvisor, and direct email debriefs to ensure all feedback is 100% authentic.

Can I speak directly with past Kewicare travelers or request references?

Yes, absolutely. We recognize that booking an African safari involves significant investment and trust. With prior permission from our alumni travelers, we regularly connect prospective guests with past clients who completed comparable itineraries—including solo female travelers, multi-generational families, or photography enthusiasts.

What makes Kewicare's guides and vehicles stand out on safari?

All Kewicare driver-guides are licensed Ugandans registered with the Uganda Safari Guides Association (USAGA) and certified by the Uganda Wildlife Authority (UWA). Our vehicles are exclusively customized 4x4 Toyota Land Cruisers equipped with heavy-duty suspension, pop-up roofs for unobstructed photography, 220V charging ports, and onboard cooler boxes.

What happens if weather, road conditions, or flight changes affect our itinerary?

Because we maintain a full-time headquarters in Kampala and regional contacts across Uganda's national parks, we monitor road and weather conditions continuously. If an international flight is delayed or weather makes a mountain road slow, our 24/7 team immediately coordinates with lodges, transfers, and parks to reschedule activities at zero extra penalty.

Are gorilla trekking permits guaranteed when I book with Kewicare?

Yes. Unlike third-party resellers, Kewicare Safaris purchases gorilla tracking permits directly from the Uganda Wildlife Authority (UWA) head office in Kampala. We secure official permit cards upon receipt of deposit and send you official confirmation details months before you travel.
