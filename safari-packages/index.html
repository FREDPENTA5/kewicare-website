Everything You Need to Know

Safari Packages FAQ

Common questions about booking, gorilla permits, vehicle options, and Uganda travel planning.

What is included in Kewicare Uganda safari packages?

Our packages are comprehensive and transparent. Every package includes private 4x4 Toyota Land Cruiser transportation with an English-speaking driver-guide, all fuel, all national park entrance fees, boat cruises, game drives, full-board lodge accommodation, and official Uganda Wildlife Authority (UWA) permits (including the $800 mountain gorilla permit and $250 chimpanzee tracking permit where applicable). We exclude international flights, Uganda tourist visas ($50 applied online), travel insurance, and personal tips.

How are gorilla trekking permits secured?

Uganda gorilla trekking permits are strictly managed and issued by the Uganda Wildlife Authority (UWA) at $800 for foreign non-residents ($700 for foreign residents). Demand is high because only 8 tourists can visit each habituated gorilla family per day. As a registered Kampala operator, Kewicare reserves and purchases your permit directly at the UWA central booking desk immediately upon deposit, securing your trek in Bwindi's Buhoma, Ruhija, Rushaga, or Nkuringo sectors. Check our Gorilla Trekking Guide for detailed preparation advice.

Can safari packages be customized for families or private groups?

Yes, absolutely. Over 70% of our guests choose customized itineraries. We adjust daily driving distances, select lodges with interconnecting family rooms or swimming pools, arrange private vehicles, and suggest alternative activities for children under the 15-year minimum age for gorilla trekking (such as guided nature walks, cultural village visits, and lake boat cruises).

What is the difference between fly-in and overland safaris?

Overland safaris travel through rural Uganda in a customized 4x4 Land Cruiser with a pop-up roof. The drive from Entebbe to Bwindi takes 8 to 9 hours through lush countryside, allowing stops at the Equator and traditional craft markets. Fly-in packages utilize scheduled domestic flights between Entebbe (EBB) and Kihihi (KHX) or Kisoro airstrips, reducing travel time to approximately 75 minutes. Learn more on our how it works page.

When is the best time of year to visit Uganda for a safari?

Uganda is great for safaris year-round because of its equatorial climate. The dry seasons from June to September and December to February offer drier forest trails for gorilla trekking and make savanna animals easier to view around waterholes in Murchison Falls and Queen Elizabeth. The wet seasons (March–May and October–November) offer lush green landscapes, superb birdwatching with migratory species, fewer crowds, and lower lodge rates.

What deposit and payment terms are required to confirm our dates?

To lock in your dates, we require a deposit covering the full cost of your non-refundable UWA gorilla/chimpanzee permits plus 30% of the remaining safari package balance. The final balance is due 30 to 45 days before your trip. Payments are processed via secure international wire transfer or major credit cards. Full cancellation policies are shared in writing with your booking voucher.
