1. About Kewicare Safaris

Kewicare Safaris is a licensed tour operator based in Kampala, Uganda, registered with the Uganda Tourism Board (UTB) and operating under the laws of the Republic of Uganda. By booking a trip with Kewicare Safaris, you agree to the following terms and conditions.

2. Booking & Confirmation

A booking is confirmed only after Kewicare Safaris has received the required deposit and sent a written confirmation via email to the address you provided. Verbal agreements or unconfirmed inquiries do not constitute a booking.

  • A 30% non-refundable deposit is required to secure your dates, gorilla/chimpanzee permits, and lodge reservations.
  • The remaining balance is due 60 days before your trip start date.
  • Bookings made within 60 days of departure require full payment at the time of booking.
  • All prices are quoted in US Dollars (USD) unless otherwise stated.

3. Payments

Payments can be made via:

  • Bank wire transfer
  • Mobile money (within Uganda)
  • Other methods agreed upon in writing

All bank charges and transfer fees are the responsibility of the traveler. Kewicare Safaris must receive the full quoted amount.

4. Gorilla & Chimpanzee Permits

Uganda Wildlife Authority (UWA) gorilla trekking permits cost $800 per person for foreign non-residents (as of 2024). Chimpanzee permits in Kibale National Park cost $250 per person. These rates are set by UWA and may change without notice.

  • Permits are non-refundable and non-transferable once issued by UWA.
  • Kewicare Safaris secures permits on your behalf but cannot guarantee availability for specific dates during peak season. We recommend booking 3–6 months in advance.
  • If a permit cannot be secured for your requested dates, Kewicare will offer alternative dates or a full refund of the permit fee.

5. Itinerary Changes

Kewicare Safaris reserves the right to modify itineraries due to:

  • Weather conditions or natural events
  • Road or park closures by government authorities
  • Safety concerns as assessed by guides or park rangers
  • Lodge or camp unavailability

In such cases, we will provide comparable alternatives at no additional cost. If you request itinerary changes after confirmation, additional fees may apply depending on lodge cancellation policies and permit availability.
